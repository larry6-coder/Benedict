Meet Benedict Wanjala

I am Benedict Wanjala, an experienced educator, curriculum specialist, mentor, and educational consultant with over eight years of experience in teaching and learner development.

My professional journey has equipped me with extensive knowledge of Competency-Based Education (CBE), curriculum implementation, learner-centered pedagogy, assessment strategies, and educational leadership.
